# Voice and Messaging

## Tone

The Vantage Foundation Uganda voice is:

- **Respectful** — communities are partners, not recipients
- **Clear** — plain language, no jargon
- **Hopeful** — focused on what's possible, not what's lacking
- **Evidence-based** — claims backed by data and sources
- **Human** — people and stories first, statistics second
- **Accountable** — transparent about what we know and don't know
- **Non-patronising** — no saviour complex
- **Accessible** — readable by a general audience
- **Concise** — every word earns its place

## Language to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "helpless victims" | "people affected by..." |
| "saving poor people" | "supporting access to..." |
| "the needy" | "communities we work with" |
| "less fortunate" | "underserved communities" |
| "giving them a voice" | "amplifying community voices" |
| "we saved / we rescued" | "we worked alongside communities to..." |
| "beneficiaries" (alone) | "community members", "participants", "residents" |
| exaggerated claims | specific, sourced numbers |
| saviour-centred messaging | community-led narratives |

## Preferred language

- "working with communities"
- "community-led"
- "supporting access to..."
- "building local capacity"
- "partnering for sustainable change"
- "people reached through..."
- "community members participated in..."
- "youth-led initiatives"
- "evidence-driven solutions"

## Messaging principles

1. **Lead with people, not statistics.** A story opens a piece; numbers support it.
2. **Be specific.** "5,000 people reached in Kasaale through the borehole project (2024, Vantage field records)" beats "thousands helped."
3. **Name the source.** Every statistic should support: source, date range, methodology, programme, verification status.
4. **Avoid vanity metrics.** Don't inflate numbers. If a number is unverified, mark it as pending or omit it.
5. **Show community agency.** Describe what community members did, not just what Vantage did for them.
6. **Be honest about limitations.** If a project is incomplete or a number is estimated, say so.

## Programme naming

Use the full programme name on first mention, then the short form:
- "Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH)" → "WASH"
- "Humanitarian Assistance" → "Humanitarian" (after first mention)
- "Youth Empowerment" → "Youth" (after first mention)

## Capitalisation

- Programme names: title case ("Health", "Education")
- Headings: sentence case preferred; title case for formal reports
- Tags/labels: lowercase in UI, title case in documents
- Avoid ALL CAPS except for short overline labels

## Numbers and units

- Use figures for statistics: "5,000 people", "12 boreholes"
- Spell out one through nine in prose; use figures for 10+
- Currency: "UGX 1,500,000" or "USD 400" — always specify the currency
- Dates: "26 July 2026" (day month year, no ordinal suffixes)

## Calls to action

- "Donate" — direct, not "Support our cause"
- "Volunteer with us" — active, not "Get involved"
- "Partner with us" — collaborative, not "Work with us"
- "Read the report" — specific, not "Learn more" (when a specific action exists)
