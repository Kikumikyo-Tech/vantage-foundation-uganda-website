## Brand personality

The identity should communicate:

- Compassionate
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Youthful
- Progressive
- Evidence-based
- Transparent
- Hopeful
- Community-led
- Resilient
- Practical
- Future-focused

## What to avoid

- Cold corporate appearance
- Overly playful, childish, or decorative appearance
- Pity-based NGO imagery or saviour-centred language
- Presenting community members as passive recipients

## Positioning

Vantage Foundation Uganda is a youth-led, community-centred, evidence-driven nonprofit that works **with** communities, governments, development partners, donors, health institutions, schools, and international organisations. The visual identity must reflect operational credibility — this is not a generic charity template; it is a distinctly African, human-centred, hopeful, and ambitious organisation.
